\textbf{Unsolicited application for a postdoctoral position on ``Wellbeing at Work''}

Dear Daphné,

I am writing to apply for a postdoctoral position on the project \textit{Wellbeing at Work: Causes and consequences of adverse working conditions}. I would work primarily on the project's third part, which studies firms' work-life balance conditions and their consequences for workers and their families.

That part of the project measures firms' work-life conditions, such as hours flexibility, schedule control, and remote work, from the texts of online vacancy postings on Jobnet using transformer-based topic modelling. It then uses within-firm changes in these conditions driven by CEO changes and mergers and acquisitions to estimate their causal effects on workers' health and labour market outcomes. It also estimates spillovers to partners' labour supply and to children's wellbeing.

I defended my PhD thesis, \textit{Meandering Through Labour Economics: Mental Well-being, Misperceptions, and Milieu in the Labour Market}, at the University of Copenhagen in September 2026. My research is in labour economics and applied microeconometrics, with a focus on job search, information frictions, gender economics, and text analysis methods applied to labour market data. I have worked with Danish administrative register data since 2019, first as a research assistant and then during my PhD. My work includes a paper on the gender gap in mental well-being, published in the \textit{Journal of International Economics}, and a paper with you both on wage misperceptions and early-career job search. I was also affiliated with the Danish Agency for Labour Market and Recruitment (STAR) from 2022 to 2026, which gave me experience with the job search and vacancy data the project relies on.

I attach my CV and would be happy to provide any further information.

Kind regards,

Asker Nygaard Christensen
